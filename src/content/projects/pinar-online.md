overview: >-
  Pınar Online was designed with the product design team, and I was responsible for the flows on this page:
  onboarding and sign-in, search and listing, product detail and profile. Grocery shopping is a weekly habit,
  so these flows are built for coming back: recent searches and categories one tap away, listings that show
  campaign badges and unit prices, focused filters, and product pages that keep the price and add to cart
  within reach while bringing recipes, health content and an AI assistant next to the product.
