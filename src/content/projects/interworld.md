description: Interworld is a cruise tour operator that sells through travel agencies. Its back office lets the team set up tours, manage ships and cabin pricing, and follow every option and reservation.
overview: >-
  Cruise cabins are sold through agencies, which hold them as time-limited options before they turn into
  reservations. I designed the back office around that cycle: a tour is built step by step (tour info, route,
  capacity and price, photos), published, and from then on every cabin option is tracked with its remaining
  time until it is confirmed or released.
